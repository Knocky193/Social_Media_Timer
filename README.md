# Social_Media_Timer
An app timer for Social Media apps with a cooldown time.

This app runs local and has 2 seperate timer. 
  one for the screentime of an app
  one for the cooldown after the first timer runs out
For blocking of the apps I use Modie und Routinen von Samsung.
Through app notifications send by the app, a routin from Modie und Routinen von Samsung
recognise the notifiation and aktivates a modie form Modie und Routinen von Samsung thats blocks
the app from using. The cooldown deactivates per notification and routin and another modie the blockades
